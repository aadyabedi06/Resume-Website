The Founder's Gambit

Enventure 2026 | St. Xavier's College, Mumbai

About

The Founder's Gambit was a dynamic, case-based entrepreneurship event designed to simulate real-world startup decision-making under pressure.

Rules

  • Participants worked in teams of 3, each having one CEO,CFO and CMO.
  • In round 1, teams would pick from 2 slots. They were presented with a startup profile and sudden crisis.
  • They had 50 minutes to strategise and prepare their presentations.
  • In round 2, teams would present one at a time. They would have a 7 minute time limit.
  • After their presentation they would be questioned by the judge for 5-10 minutes.

About the Judge

The event was judged by Mr. Krishna Kanhaiya, Director and CEO of Mirae Asset Financial Services(India).

What did I learn?

Participating in this fest by curating my own event was super stressful yet so insightful. I learned so many new things in the process. I was very grateful to be surrounded with such a good team, every member was very hardworking as well as helpful. This was a completely new experience for me and I am grateful for it as it gave me a lot of practical knowledge. One of the main highlights of the event was speaking to Mr. Krishna who had some valuable insights to share from his own experience in the industry.
