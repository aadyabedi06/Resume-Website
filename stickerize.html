Stickerize

Venturing on a Budget |BBA DBE T2 Course

About The Project

  • For the course- Venturing on a Budget in term 2 of the BBA DBE program, I was supposed to design a venture under the budget of 250 rupees.
  • My venture was called Stickerize. It specialized in stickers and posters.
  • "Post it. Stick it. Own it."" was the tagline for stickerize.

Venture Overview

  • Business Model: B2C
  • Mode: Online Business
  • Target Audience: College Students
  • Time Constraint: 30 minutes

Finance

  • Total Investment: ₹140
  • Total Revenue: ₹160
  • Net Profit: ₹20

Learning

This venture taught me multiple things. I learnt how to price strategically, how to indentify a niche market, how to execute under time pressure, etc. Most importantly, it reinforced that entrepreneurship in not about large investments but about recognizing opportunities, leveraging available resources and taking calculated risks. The project strengthened my confidence in my ability to conceptualize, execute and evaluate a business idea with real-world constraints.
